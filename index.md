# Arduino Light-Tracking Platform Check-in 1
Riley Gardner

I built a platform that turns to follow a flashlight. Two photoresistors
measure the light on each side of a cardboard divider. The Arduino compares
the readings and moves the servo toward the brighter side. When the
readings are close, the platform holds its position.
